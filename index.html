Teacher Notes & Sources

How the maths works: speed = distance ÷ time. The 100 m comparison and planet race assume each racer could keep one constant speed. Your result is an average over the measured run; the athlete and cheetah figures are illustrative peak speeds, and the jet figure is approximate. Light's speed in a vacuum is exact.

Planet distances use approximate average orbital distances. One astronomical unit (AU) is 149,597,870,700 metres.
