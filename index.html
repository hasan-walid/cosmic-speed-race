Teacher Notes & Sources

How the maths works: speed = distance ÷ time. The 100 m comparison and planet race assume each racer keeps one constant speed. The student's result is a measured average. Athlete and cheetah values represent brief peaks; the jet value is approximate. Light's speed in a vacuum is exact.

Speed sources: The page keeps its original illustrative Mbappé benchmark of 10.89 m/s; it is not a verified measurement. UEFA measured 36.7 km/h (about 10.19 m/s) at EURO 2024. The IUCN assessment documents cheetahs reaching 29 m/s. The Smithsonian reports an SR-71 record of 3,529.6 km/h (about 980.4 m/s), close to this page's approximate 980.56 m/s.

Planet distances use approximate average orbital distances. One astronomical unit (AU) is 149,597,870,700 metres.
